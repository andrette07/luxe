Philosophy

Beauty is a conversation—
held in low light, at an unhurried tempo,
between an artist and the person in her chair.

I
Quiet Luxury

Restraint over excess. A space designed for pause — where every detail is intentional, from the grain of the marble to the tempo of the music.

II
Science & Soul

Clinical efficacy meets ritual. Our protocols are rooted in evidence, performed with the patience of an artisan.

III
Made by Hand

No conveyor belts, no shortcuts. Small bookings, unhurried hours, and artists who remember your name, your skin, your story.

The Founder

Ashley
McDavid.

Ashley founded Luxe on a simple idea — that beauty work should feel like a visit to a tailor: quiet, specific, and entirely about the person in the room.

Luxe is a house of hair, skin, aesthetics, and wellness services practiced side by side — with a menu edited by hand.
